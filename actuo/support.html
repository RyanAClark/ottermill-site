Support

Fastest way to reach us: support@ottermill.app. We aim to reply within a few business days.

Where is my data stored?

On your device. Actuo is local-first: your plans, streaks, and progress never leave your phone, and no account is required. Deleting the app deletes its data.

How do I restore a Premium purchase?

Open the Premium screen and tap Restore purchases while signed in to the same App Store / Google Play account you bought with.

How do I cancel a subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by your store account, not by us: Settings → Manage subscription in the app, or your device's App Store / Google Play subscription settings. Cancelling stops future renewals; access continues until the period ends.

Reminders aren't firing

Check that notifications are allowed for Actuo in your device settings, and that a reminder time is set in the app under Settings.
